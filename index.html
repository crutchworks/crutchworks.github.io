When the engineering must be right the first time.

Procurement is demanding. Equipment must perform under extreme load conditions, comply with rigorous standards, and be independently verified before they are fielded. The cost of a structural failure in a military context — to personnel, equipment, and mission — is unacceptable.

Crutchworks provides the independent mechanical engineering rigour that defence contractors, prime suppliers, and government procurement teams require. As a chartered engineer with deep FEA and mechanical design experience, Andrew Crutchley brings the technical depth of a consultancy with the accountability and responsiveness of a specialist practitioner.

01

Defence Engineering Services

Structural FEA

Finite Element Analysis

Using ANSYS Mechanical, we perform detailed stress, strain, fatigue and deformation analysis on military and heavy-industry components. FEA allows the analysis of complex geometry and loading scenarios that are not possible with manual calculations — including dynamic shock, impact, and vibration — to be assessed without costly physical prototyping. Reports are produced to support regulatory sign-off and procurement requirements.

Protective Structures

ROPS / FOPS / OPS Design & Certification

We design and certify Operator Protective Structures for military and heavy vehicles, including Rollover Protective Structures (ROPS), Falling Object Protective Structures (FOPS), and Operator Protective Structures (OPS). Designs are validated against ISO 3471, ISO 8082, AS/NZS standards and WorkSafe NZ requirements — critical for any NZDF vehicle or plant procurement.

Design & CAD

Mechanical Design & Autodesk Inventor

From concept through to detailed design, we produce 3D models and engineering drawings using Autodesk Inventor. Clients receive design packages suitable for manufacture, procurement documentation, and long-term asset management. We work alongside your existing design teams or independently as lead designer.

Standards Compliance

Code & Standards Verification

Structural designs are assessed against applicable New Zealand and Australian standards including AS3990, AS4100 and AS1418. For defence work equipment can be assessed against standards such as STANAG 4569 and can produce verification documentation to support formal procurement processes.

Independent Review

Third-Party Design Review

Providing an independent engineering review of designs submitted by prime contractors or OEM suppliers. We assess structural adequacy, identify design risk, and produce written reports that procurement teams can rely on. Independent verification is increasingly required under NZ Government procurement rules for defence-related acquisitions.

Project Delivery

Engineering Project Management

For projects requiring mechanical engineering oversight from concept to field deployment, we provide project management support — managing scope, schedule, technical risk, and stakeholder communication. Our experience spans mining, heavy industry, and defence environments where programme discipline and technical rigour must coexist.

Technical Capability in Detail

Primary Tool — ANSYS Mechanical

Finite Element Analysis

FEA is the backbone of modern mechanical engineering for defence applications. Where physical testing is cost-prohibitive or operationally impossible, FEA delivers verified performance data. We use ANSYS Mechanical and LS Dyna — the industry standard for high-consequence structural and mechanical analysis — to evaluate components and systems across a wide range of scenarios.

Capabilities include static structural analysis, fatigue and lifecycle assessment, ballistic and blast simulation, dynamic loading and shock analysis. Our FEA outputs are documented to a standard suitable for regulatory submission, procurement assurance, and design authority approval.

For specialist requirements, access to LS-DYNA (for crash and impact simulation) and nCode (for durability and fatigue) is available, enabling full lifecycle and survivability assessment.

Analysis Types Available
  • Static & quasi-static structural analysis
  • Fatigue & lifecycle durability
  • Dynamic loading & shock response
  • Ballistic Analysis
  • Buckling & stability analysis
  • Blast and Explosion Simulation
  • Crash & impact (LS-DYNA)
  • Vibration & modal analysis
Design & Certification

Operator Protective Structures

The design and certification of ROPS, FOPS, and OPS for military vehicles and plant equipment is a specialist discipline. Structures must not only pass physical or analytical test criteria — they must be certified by a suitably qualified engineer and documented to a standard that satisfies regulatory and procurement requirements.

We design protective structures for light and heavy vehicles, conduct FEA-based Grade 2 certification analysis, and produce full certification packages. Whether you are procuring new vehicles for the NZDF, modifying existing plant, or need a review of a contractor-supplied design, Crutchworks can provide the independent engineering sign-off your project requires.

Relevant Standards
  • ISO 3471 — ROPS for Earth-Moving Machinery
  • ISO 8082 — ROPS for Forestry Machinery
  • WorkSafe NZ Operator Protective Structures Code
  • AS3990 — Mechanical Equipment (Steel)
